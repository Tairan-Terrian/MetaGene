The quality of the RNA was analyzed using the Agilent Bioanalyzer 2100 and the RNA 6000 LabChip Kit (Agilent) with the Eukaryote Total RNA Nano Assay, with RIN ranging between 9.2 and 9.8.
!Sample_label_ch1 = Cy3
!Sample_label_protocol_ch1 = For each sample 100 ng of total RNA were reverse transcribed, amplified by in vitro transcription and labelled with Cy3-CTP using the One-color Microarray-Based Gene Expression Analysis  protocol (Version 6.6, September 2012, Agilent) with the low input quick Amp Labeling kit following the manufacturer's instructions. One-color RNA Spike (Agilent) was included for estimation of limit of detection and dynamic range (2 µl of 1:10,000 dilution per sample). After denaturation 10 minutes at 65ºC with oligonucleotide priomers reverse transcription into cDNA was perfomed by incubating for 2h at 40ºC, followed by heat inactivation 15 minutes at 70ºC, in vitro transcription into fluorescently tagged cRNA 2 hours at 40ºC, and cleanup of unincorporated label on RNAeasy columns (Qiagen). Labelled sample quality was assessed by spectrophotometry fullfilling manufacturer specifications (specific activity>6pmol/ug Cy3) .
!Sample_hyb_protocol = For each sample, 825 ng of Cy3 labeled cRNA were hybridized in an Agilent Hyb Chamber for 17 h at 65ºC in a rotisserie oven with 10 rpm rotation speed in 0.5X fragmentation buffer, 1X Agilent Gene Expression hybridization buffer, 1X blocking agent. Each labeled sample was hybridized on a separate subarray of 8-plex in situ synthesized oligonucleotide probe microarray slides (SurePrint G3 HumanV2 GE 8x60K Microarrays, Agilent microarray design ID: 039494). The day after , slides were disassembled and washed first in Gene Expression Wash Buffer 1 with 0.005% Triton X102 for 1 minute at room temperature, followed by a second wash in Gene Expression Wash Buffer 2 with 0.05% Triton X102 for 1 minute at 37º C followed by scanning in an Agilent scanner.
!Sample_scan_protocol = The Agilent Scan Control software was used to control de scanner and generate .tif images using default conditions for 8x60K G3 Microarray format (AgilentG3_GX_1Color: Green Dye channel, Scan Area 61 x 21.6 mm, Scan resolution 3 μm,  20 bit Tiff format (dynamic range of intensities from 1 to 1048576). The raw data as tabulated text files were obtained with Agilent Feature Extraction software version 10.7.3.1 with the following settings (annotation version: 039494_D_F_20140813, extraction protocol: GE1_107_Sep09).
!Sample_description = Single treatment, derived oxaliplatin resistant HTOXAR3 colorectal cancer cell line, treated with oxaliplatin, biological replicate 2 of 3
!Sample_data_processing = Raw data intensities were background corrected using normexp, and log2intensity distributions were made comparable between samples using quantile normalization. Values were collapsed using the aggregate function in R by common gene identifier and control probes were exluded from the statistical analysis. Differential gene expression was assessed by fitting to an empirical Bayesian linear model. Statistical significance in differential gene expression was determined by setting a false discovery rate applying the Benjamini and Yekutiely method for multiple testing adjustment correction. For analysis we used the Limma package and the R statistical programming environment (Smyth, 2005).
