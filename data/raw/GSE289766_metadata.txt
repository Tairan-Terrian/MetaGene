!Series_summary = Although anticancer drugs such as fluorouracil and oxaliplatin are widely used in the treatment of colorectal cancer, they are ineffective when cancer cells acquire drug resistance. The treatment strategy for colorectal cancer that has acquired drug resistance has become a problem in chemotherapy. To investigate the cause of drug resistance, we created drug-resistant strains of colon cancer cell lines by mixing a small amount of anticancer drug in the culture medium and culturing them for a long period of time, and explored the genetic changes by RNA-sequence.
!Series_overall_design = The colorectal cancer cell lines HCT116, SW480, and DLD1 were exposed to a non-lethal dose of fluorouracil(5 - 70μM) and oxaliplatin(5 - 35μM) and cultured for about 6 months for RNA-seq analysis.
